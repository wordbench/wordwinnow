# Reference word lists

Two CEFR-annotated English word lists ship with the project unchanged.

They are the external labels every level assessment and every model evaluation
is measured against.

The files are kept exactly as published, and the reader takes from them the
single-word entries in the four open word classes, as [`docs/domain.md`](../../docs/domain.md) sets
out.

## CEFR-J Vocabulary Profile, version 1.5

File: `cefrj-vocabulary-profile-1.5.csv` (7,799 entries, levels A1 to B2).

Columns: `headword`, `pos`, `CEFR`, `CoreInventory 1`, `CoreInventory 2`, `Threshold`.

Copyright: Tono Laboratory, Tokyo University of Foreign Studies.

Terms of use, as published by the distributor: "CEFR-J vocabulary and grammar
profile datasets can be used for research and commercial purposes with no
charge, provided that you cite the dataset properly."

Citation: The CEFR-J Wordlist Version 1.5. Compiled by Yukio Tono, Tokyo
University of Foreign Studies.

Retrieved from <http://www.cefr-j.org/download.html> on 1/20/2020.

Obtained from <https://github.com/openlanguageprofiles/olp-en-cefrj> at
`2026-10-01T00:00:00Z`, midnight UTC at the start of October 1, 2026.

The distributor also offers a later version, 1.6, dated 2020-03-24; the
repository this project took the list from ships version 1.5, and that is the
one used here.
